{{- /* Parses the gallery shortcodes out of a page's raw Markdown and returns a slice of photos, each a map with src (thumbnail, as written), full (lightbox URL, see smug-full.html) and title. Source order is kept. Matches the gallery shortcode in both its angle-bracket and percent forms, with double-quoted src and title arguments; the opening galleries wrapper does not match, and neither do escaped (commented) examples. Usage: {{ $photos := partial "photos.html" $page }} */ -}} {{- $out := slice -}} {{- range findRESubmatch `\{\{[<%]\s*gallery\s([^}]*)\}\}` .RawContent -}} {{- $args := index . 1 -}} {{- $src := "" -}} {{- $title := "" -}} {{- with findRESubmatch `\bsrc="([^"]*)"` $args 1 -}}{{- $src = index (index . 0) 1 -}}{{- end -}} {{- with findRESubmatch `\btitle="([^"]*)"` $args 1 -}}{{- $title = index (index . 0) 1 -}}{{- end -}} {{- if $src -}} {{- $out = $out | append (dict "src" $src "full" (partial "smug-full.html" $src) "title" $title) -}} {{- end -}} {{- end -}} {{- return $out -}}
